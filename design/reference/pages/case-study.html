01 ◆ WHAT WE FOUND

Dispatch ran on memory.

Eleven branches shared one WhatsApp group and three spreadsheets. It worked because Khalid, the dispatch lead, remembered everything. When he went on leave, dispatch time tripled.

We spent two weeks in the Riyadh and Dammam warehouses. We didn't find a software problem; we found a handoff problem that software could hold.

BEFORE
  • Orders pasted into WhatsApp, re-typed into Excel
  • Drivers called in to confirm delivery
  • Invoices built by hand at month end
AFTER
  • Orders arrive from the ERP, routed by rule
  • Drivers photograph the signed note in the app
  • Invoices draft themselves; finance approves in one tap

"It feels like the system was built by someone who worked here."Khalid Al-Harbi, Head of Dispatch
