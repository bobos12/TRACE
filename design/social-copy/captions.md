Two weeks of posts, three a week. Post on LinkedIn first (Tuesday–Thursday,
8–10am ET works best for US B2B), then Instagram the same day.

Hashtags go at the end on LinkedIn (3 max) and Instagram (5–8). Don't use
them on X.

## Rules of thumb

- One idea per post. Short sentences.
- Never post a client's work, logo or numbers without their written OK.
- Never invent results. If it isn't measured, don't post a number.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour.
