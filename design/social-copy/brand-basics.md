# Brand basics

For anyone making a new post or graphic by hand (Canva, Figma, Keynote).

## Colours

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carbon | `#0F0F0D` | Main background for social |
| Paper | `#F4F1E9` | Light background, text on carbon |
| Vermilion | `#FF5A33` | The nuqta and one call to action — never large areas of text |
| Vermilion (on paper) | `#E0461F` | Vermilion on light backgrounds |
| Graphite | `#1A1A17` | Raised panels on carbon |
| Ash | `#A7A193` | Secondary text on carbon |
| Ash (on paper) | `#5C584E` | Secondary text on paper |

## Type

- **Instrument Sans**, SemiBold (600), tight letter-spacing (−3 to −4%) for headlines.
- **IBM Plex Mono**, Medium (500), UPPERCASE with wide spacing for small labels
  ("SOFTWARE STUDIO ◆ USA · CAIRO").
- Both are free on Google Fonts. The font files are also in the website project
  under `public/fonts/`.

## The signature elements

- **The nuqta** — the vermilion rhombus. Use it as the full stop at the end of a
  headline, and as a bullet. One per area: if everything is marked, nothing is.
- **The symbol** — three nuqtas, the top one vermilion. The profile picture.
- **The cut** — a 45° corner cut off the top-right of a panel or button.
- **The constellation** — small rhombus dots joined by thin lines.

## Don'ts

- No stock photos, gradients, glassy blur effects, emoji in graphics, or 3D shapes.
- Don't stretch, recolour or re-letter the wordmark. Use the files in `05-logos/`.
- Don't put the wordmark on a busy image — carbon or paper behind it.
