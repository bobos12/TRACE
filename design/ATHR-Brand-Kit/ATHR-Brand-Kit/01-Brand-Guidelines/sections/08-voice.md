# Voice

**Confident, intelligent, clear, human, direct.** We sound like the people who will actually build the thing.

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| Revolutionizing the digital landscape | We replace the spreadsheet your team is afraid to touch. |
| Cutting-edge innovative solutions | A portal, a driver app and a sync with your ERP. |
| Empowering businesses through next-gen technology | Your branch managers approve invoices from their phones. |
| We are passionate about excellence | We measure dispatch time before and after. It went from 40 minutes to 6. |
| Seamless, end-to-end digital transformation | We start in your warehouse, not in our codebase. |

## Rules

- Lead with their business, then our work, then the technology (if at all).
- Specific over impressive: names, numbers, days of the week.
- Short sentences. One idea each. Full stop — or the nuqta.
- Admit limits: "Sometimes the answer isn't software. We'll say so."
- Buttons are verb + object. Errors say what happened and what to do.

## Arabic

Written in clear Modern Standard Arabic, warm and direct — not a translation of the English and not government prose. Use نحن and أنتم/أنت consistently; prefer short verbal sentences. Egyptian or Gulf dialect only in social copy aimed at that market.

> نفهم عملك. نبني حوله. ونترك أثرًا.
