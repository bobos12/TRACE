# The ATHR signature

Someone should be able to see a single element and think *that's ATHR*. That element is **the nuqta placed with intent** — and it has four forms:

| Form | Where | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The full stop** | Display headlines, slide titles, social posts: "We build around it◆" | Only on display sizes; only once per view; `at-stop` |
| **The marker** | Active nav, tab, sidebar item, selected radio, current step, timeline event | Marks *the one thing* that is current or chosen |
| **The cut** | Primary button, featured/case cards, proposal page corners, app icon | Trailing top corner, 45°; mirrors in RTL |
| **The constellation** | Client portals, case studies, proposal covers, handovers, empty states | One arrangement per client, generated from the name, never reused |

Together they tell the brand story without words: we study the skeleton, we build it, and we place the mark that makes it someone's.

## The editorial baseline

In documents a hairline in `ink` runs across a section and ends in a vermilion nuqta — the line that continues after the word. Use it once per page, under a section's first paragraph or above a timeline.
