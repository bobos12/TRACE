# Phase 1 — Brand strategy

## Core idea

**The dot that makes it yours.** In Arabic, ب ت ث share one skeleton — the *rasm*. What makes ث a ث is the three dots placed above it. Businesses are the same: most need similar software skeletons (accounts, orders, approvals, dashboards). What makes a system *theirs* is how it's shaped around their people, their language and their workflow. ATHR builds the skeleton properly and places the mark.

## Brand essence

**أثر — the mark that remains.** A trace, an imprint, an effect, what's left after you've gone. For ATHR it has three concrete readings:

| Meaning | What it asks of ATHR |
| --- | --- |
| Trace — we study how work actually moves | Discovery before design; we visit before we quote |
| Imprint — the result is uniquely theirs | Custom, bilingual, shaped to the workflow; no templates |
| Effect — it changes something measurable | We measure before and after, and say the number |

## Positioning

For businesses in Egypt and the Gulf that have outgrown spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and off-the-shelf tools, **ATHR is the software studio that learns how your business works and builds the system around it** — in Arabic and English, measured by what it changes. Unlike agencies that sell templates or dev shops that sell hours, ATHR sells a result that carries the client's own mark.

**Proof points:** two-week discovery inside the business · Arabic and English from day one · fixed price per phase, stop any time and keep the code · every project measured and handed over.

## Design principles

1. **Mark, don't decorate.** Every brand element marks something — a selection, a result, an ending, an owner. If it marks nothing, remove it.
2. **One mark per place.** One vermilion nuqta per view region; one primary action; one inverse card per row.
3. **Both scripts, one system.** Arabic is designed, not translated: its own sizes, its own leading, mirrored layout.
4. **Structure before style.** Grids, rules and type carry the page; colour is the last 5%.
5. **Theirs, not ours.** Client products lead with the client's name and constellation; ATHR signs small at the foot.
6. **Measured.** Every claim has a number, every layout has a unit.
