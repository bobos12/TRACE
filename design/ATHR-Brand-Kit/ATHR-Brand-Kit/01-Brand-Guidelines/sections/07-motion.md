# Motion

Motion is the mark being made. Minimal, fast, purposeful.

| Move | What it does | Tokens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stamp** | A nuqta arrives slightly large and presses down into place — confirms an action | `duration-trace`, `ease-mark` |
| **Trace** | A hairline draws from start to end; the toast timer runs out along its base | `duration-trace`, `ease-trace` |
| **Form** | Panels and modals appear with a short clip-reveal, as if being cut out | `duration-trace`, `ease-mark` |
| **Assemble** | The loader: three nuqtas taking turns to form ث | 1.2s loop |
| **Press** | Buttons move 1px down on press | `duration-press` |
| **Move** | Tab and nav markers slide to the new item | `duration-quick` |

Rules: nothing bounces, nothing floats, no parallax, no scroll-jacking. Marketing reveals may use `duration-reveal` once per section. Respect reduced-motion.
