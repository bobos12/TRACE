PHASE 2 ◆ CREATIVE DIRECTIONS

Six ways to leave a mark

Each direction reads أثر differently: as a dot, a line, a pressure, a trail, a system, a seal. Two survive — the nuqta as the mark, the constellation as the system — and borrow the cut and the baseline from their neighbours.

A · The Nuqtaالنقطة

ث is ت with one more dot. The letters share a skeleton; the dots decide which letter it is. Three rhombic nuqtas sit over the TH in ATHR and over the ث in أثر — the same mark, over the same sound, in both scripts. Arranged ∴, they also read as the logician's "therefore": consequence, effect — another meaning of أثر.

Chosen The only idea that is bilingual by construction, not by translation. Scales from a 16px favicon to a building sign.

B · The Baselineالسطر

Arabic is written on a line that keeps going after the word ends. The wordmark stands on a baseline that runs past the R and ends in a single dot — the work continues after we leave.

Borrowed Too close to a signature-underline cliché as a logo; kept as the editorial hairline in documents and proposals.

C · The Cutالأثر المطبوع

An impression is what's missing after pressure. One corner of every ATHR surface is pressed out at 45° — the angle of the nuqta's side. The brand is the absence, not an added ornament.

Borrowed Weak as a standalone logo (any chamfered box). Strong as a UI signature: primary buttons, feature cards, the app icon.

D · The Trailالأثر المتحرك

A form leaving a trail as it becomes another form: the rhombus settles into a square, before → after. Business transformation as a single gesture.

Motion only Reads as "fintech arrow" when static. Lives on as the loader and page-transition logic.

E · The Constellationالبصمة

A 3×3 lattice of nuqtas. ATHR's own mark is the ث arrangement; every client project gets its own arrangement, generated once from its name and never reused. Their portal, their case study, their proposal cover carry it.

Chosen "Every solution leaves its own mark" made literal without a fingerprint. One grammar, unlimited marks.

F · The Sealالختم

A khatam: the seal that leaves an imprint and certifies the work. Square, heavy-bordered, bilingual. Strong on stationery, handover documents and a "built by ATHR" footer.

Parked Pulls toward heritage and bureaucracy. Kept for one use: the handover certificate.
