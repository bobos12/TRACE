# NavBar

The marketing header: wordmark, up to five links, the language switch, one CTA.

- `links` (strings or `{label, href, current}`), `lang` (the other language's name: "عربي" / "EN"), `cta` (label), `arabic` (uses the Arabic wordmark; wrap in `dir="rtl"`).
- The current page carries a nuqta, not a colour change.
