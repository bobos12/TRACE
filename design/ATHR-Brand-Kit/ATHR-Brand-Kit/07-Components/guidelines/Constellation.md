# Constellation

A 3×3 lattice of nuqtas — each client project's own mark, generated deterministically from its name.

- `seed`: the client or project name (same name → same mark, always). `ATHR` or `أثر` returns the ث arrangement. `size` = one cell in px; `quiet` hides the empty cells.
- Use on the client's portal header, their case study, their proposal cover and their handover certificate. It is theirs: don't reuse a seed for a different client, don't hand-edit arrangements.
- Colours come from `ink` and `vermilion`; to set it on a dark band, override `--ink` on the wrapper.
