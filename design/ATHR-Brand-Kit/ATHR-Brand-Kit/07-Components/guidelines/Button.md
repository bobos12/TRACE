# Button

One primary per view: the action the screen exists for.

- `variant`: `primary` (nuqta fill with the cut corner — the brand's pressed-out 45°) · `ink` (high emphasis without the accent, used in navs and on marketing CTAs) · `secondary` (default) · `ghost` · `danger`. `size`: `sm` · `md` · `lg`. `icon` / `iconEnd` take an Icon name; arrows flip in RTL. `cut` forces or removes the chamfer. `href` renders a link.
- Labels: a verb and an object, sentence case — "Approve invoice", "Start a project". Never "Submit", "Click here", "Learn more".
- Danger buttons appear only inside a confirmation, never as the first thing on a page.
