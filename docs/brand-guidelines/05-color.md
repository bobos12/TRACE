# Colour directions

Four palettes were tested before choosing.

| Direction | Palette | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ink & vermilion** ◆ chosen | carbon `#0F0F0D`, paper `#F4F1E9`, sand `#D8D1C0`, vermilion `#E0461F` | Timeless, warm, owns one colour. The red has a reason: early Arabic manuscripts inked dots and vowel marks in red. |
| Charcoal & oxide | charcoal, ivory, oxidised copper | Premium but reads "craft brewery"; weak in dashboards |
| Desert & signal | clay, sand, one electric green | Too literal-regional; green collides with success |
| Monochrome editorial | pure black/white, no accent | Elegant in print, lifeless in product; no way to mark state |

## Roles

- **Carbon** — authority; the dark theme, covers, app icon.
- **Paper** — calm, warm reading ground; never pure white.
- **Sand / ash** — relief blocks, secondary chart series, image mats.
- **Vermilion** — the mark. Graphic only, ≤5% of any surface.
- **Nuqta** (`#B8330F` / `#FF6B47`) — the accessible, interactive version of vermilion.
- **Status** — blue-teal success, ochre warning, crimson-rose danger; never confused with the brand red because danger always carries an icon and sits in its own hue family.

Charts: series 1 `ink`, series 2 `ash`/`line-strong`, highlight a single point in `vermilion`. No rainbow palettes.
