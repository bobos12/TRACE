# Phase 2 — Creative directions

Six directions were explored; the **Directions** page shows each one drawn. Every direction answers the same eight questions.

## A · The Nuqta — النقطة ◆ chosen as the mark

1. **Concept** — ث is ت plus a mark. The brand is the mark.
2. **Metaphor** — the three dots of ث; also ∴, "therefore": consequence, effect.
3. **Why ATHR** — the only concept that is bilingual by construction: the cluster sits over the same sound (TH / ث) in both scripts.
4. **Construction** — three squares rotated 45° (calligraphic nuqtas are rhombic), laid on a diagonal lattice with a gap of 0.36 × the half-diagonal. Latin: centred over the T–H pair, one cluster-height above cap height. Arabic: replaces the font's own dots on a dotless ٮ.
5. **Type** — Instrument Sans SemiBold, +36 units tracking; IBM Plex Sans Arabic SemiBold.
6. **Favicon / app icon** — the cluster alone on carbon; legible at 16px because the three rhombi never merge.
7. **Black & white** — fully works; the colour version only inks the dots vermilion.
8. **System** — becomes the nuqta (single rhombus), the loader (dots forming ث) and the constellation lattice.

## B · The Baseline — السطر ◆ borrowed

1. Arabic sits on a line that continues after the word. 2. A line that keeps going after the work ends. 3. Continuity, legacy. 4. Wordmark on a hairline that runs past the R and ends in a nuqta. 5. Same Latin face. 6. Fails as an icon (a line is not a shape). 7. Works. 8. **Kept as** the editorial hairline in proposals and documents — a rule that ends in a vermilion nuqta.

## C · The Cut — الأثر المطبوع ◆ borrowed

1. An impression is the absence left by pressure. 2. A corner pressed out at 45°. 3. The imprint as subtraction, not ornament. 4. Chamfer = 1 nuqta-angle, sized to the element (10px buttons, 20–36px cards). 5. Any. 6. The app icon keeps a cut corner on print, and the OS rounds it on screens. 7. Works. 8. **Kept as** the UI signature on primary buttons, featured cards, proposal pages. Too generic alone to be the logo.

## D · The Trail — الأثر المتحرك ◆ motion only

1. Before → after. 2. A rhombus leaving a trail as it settles into a square. 3. Transformation. 4. Five steps of rotation and opacity. 5. Any. 6. Unreadable at icon size. 7. Loses its meaning without opacity. 8. **Kept as** motion logic: the loader, page transitions, the toast trace.

## E · The Constellation — البصمة ◆ chosen as the system

1. Every solution leaves its own mark. 2. A 3×3 lattice where each client's name lights a different arrangement. 3. Customisation made visible, without a fingerprint. 4. Nine rhombic cells; 3–5 filled, one vermilion; deterministic from the name. ATHR's own = ث. 5. Mono labels. 6. Works as a client portal favicon. 7. Works. 8. Client portals, case studies, proposal covers, handover certificates, empty states.

## F · The Seal — الختم ◆ parked

1. A khatam certifies and leaves an imprint. 2. A square seal with the cluster and ATHR · أثر. 3. Authorship. 4. Double-ruled square. 5. Tracked caps. 6. Too detailed at 16px. 7. Works. 8. **Kept for one use:** the handover certificate. Pulls toward heritage and bureaucracy as a primary identity.

## Phase 3 — Selection

**A + E, with C and B as supporting gestures.** The nuqta cluster is the mark; the constellation is how the mark multiplies for clients; the cut and the baseline give the UI and documents an ownable signature without adding a single decorative element. D survives as motion; F as one artefact.
