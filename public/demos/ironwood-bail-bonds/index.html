Questions

What families ask us first.

Still unsure? Call us — a licensed agent will answer, any hour.

How much does a bail bond cost in Texas?

The premium is usually 10–15% of the bond amount, set by the county. On a $5,000 bond that is typically $500–$750, which we can split into payments.

How long does release take?

Once the bond is posted, most releases from Harris County take 3–6 hours, depending on how busy the jail is.

Do I need to come to your office?

No. You can sign and pay from your phone. Our office is open 24/7 if you prefer to meet in person.

What if they miss a court date?

Call us right away. We send reminders before every hearing and help reschedule when possible.
